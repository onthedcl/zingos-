# The offer

**AI Visibility Audit — $149, delivered in 48 hours.**

> You know your Google rank. You have no idea whether AI assistants recommend
> you. I'll measure it: 20 real buyer questions, asked 100 times, scored for
> whether you get named and who gets named instead.

## Why this sells right now

- The anxiety is live and unbudgeted-for. Every B2B marketing leader has been
  asked "are we showing up in ChatGPT?" and has no answer.
- It is **measurable but not yet measured.** Rank tracking is a solved,
  commoditised purchase. This is not.
- The deliverable is a number they can forward to their CEO. That is what they
  are actually buying.
- No incumbent owns the category at the $149 price point. The monitoring
  platforms start around $400/mo and demand an annual commitment — far too
  heavy for "I just need to know where we stand."

## Pricing ladder

| Tier | Price | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| Teaser | free | Hero number + top 3 blind spots. The outreach hook. |
| **Audit** | **$149** | Full report: 20 questions x 5 runs, competitor set, blind spots |
| Quarterly | $99/qtr | Same audit re-run, with movement vs. last quarter |

Start at $149. It is high enough to signal seriousness, low enough to clear a
manager's discretionary spend without procurement. Raise it after five sales —
if nobody flinches, it is priced too low.

## Unit economics

~100 model calls per audit, ~1k in / 800 out each. On Sonnet 5 at low effort
that is roughly **$1–2 of tokens**, plus a few cents for question generation.
At $149, the margin is ~99%. The ratio, not the price, is what makes this
survive contact with the ledger.

The teaser is the only real cost risk: it burns tokens with no guaranteed sale.
Cap it — run teasers in batches, on Haiku, at 3 runs instead of 5.

## Why it stays defensible for a while

The moat is not the code, which is a weekend's work. It is knowing *which 20
questions matter* in a given category — that is the domain expertise, and it
does not transfer to a generic tool.

## Objections, pre-answered

**"Doesn't this change every time the model updates?"** Yes. That is the
argument for the quarterly tier, not against the audit. Say so plainly.

**"You only tested one model."** True, and the report says so in the method
note. Testing more is a straightforward upgrade once one buyer asks for it —
don't build it before then.

**"Can't I just ask ChatGPT myself?"** They can, once. The product is the
*rate* across 100 samples, which is the difference between an anecdote and a
measurement. Lead with that.
